Applied Statistics

Mean vs. Median: Choosing the Right Central Tendency

The mean (average) sums all data points and divides by count, making it highly vulnerable to extreme outliers.

Salaries: $50k, $52k, $48k, $55k, $1,200,000
Mean is ~$281k (distorted) | Median is $52k (accurate)

For skewed data (compensation, latency, home values), prioritize the Median.
